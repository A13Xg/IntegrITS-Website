# Executive Summary: Integrits Corporation

**Integrits Corporation** is a Veteran-Owned Small Business (VOSB) founded in 2000 that specializes in delivering business, engineering, and technology solutions to government and commercial clients. Headquartered in San Diego, California, the company emphasizes long-term organizational experience, employee retention, and high-quality customer service.

---

## 1. Company Overview

* **Entity Name:** Integrits Corporation
* **Business Classification:** Veteran-Owned Small Business (VOSB)
* **Year Established:** 2000 (20+ years in operation)
* **Motto / Core Value:** *"Turning Technology into Solutions"* — focused on superior customer service, cost-effective approaches, and quality results.
* **Corporate Culture:** Prioritizes a work-life balance culture, promoting high employee capacity, integrity, and long-term performance.

## 2. Primary Capabilities & Service Offerings

Integrits Corporation structures its core competencies into three main domain areas:

### A. Range Engineering & Technical Services
Operation, maintenance, and modernization of range instrumentation and control systems, including:
* **Radars & Telemetry**
* **Optics & Safety Systems**
* **Aircraft TSPI / ACMI** (Time Space Position Information / Air Combat Maneuvering Instrumentation)
* **Communications & Information Services**
* **Mission Control**

### B. Program Management Services
End-to-end management and administrative expertise to support client operations:
* High-level program management and strategy
* Policy development
* Administrative staffing and operational oversight

### C. Information Technology & Cybersecurity Services
Enterprise-level IT architecture design, management, and security, encompassing:
* Network & infrastructure engineering
* Cybersecurity implementation & monitoring
* Computer operations & user support
* Hardware & software maintenance
* Network and facilities management
