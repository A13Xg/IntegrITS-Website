# IntegrITS Corporation — Website Intelligence Report

**Source scope:** This report uses only pages hosted on `integrits.com`. External recruiting pages and third-party sources were not accessed.  
**Review date:** August 5, 2026

## 1. Executive Summary

IntegrITS Corporation presents itself as a **veteran-owned small business founded in 2000**, headquartered in San Diego, California. Its core market is professional and technical services, with a particularly strong emphasis on U.S. Department of Defense operations, test ranges, military installations, systems engineering, program support, enterprise IT, and cybersecurity.

The company organizes its capabilities into three principal service areas:

1. **Range Engineering and Technical Services**
2. **Program Management**
3. **Information Technology and Cybersecurity**

The strongest differentiator visible on the website is IntegrITS’ combination of operational range expertise, flight-test instrumentation knowledge, DoD acquisition familiarity, program-management services, and enterprise IT support. Its published capabilities extend from hands-on equipment maintenance and range operations through engineering, test planning, cybersecurity authorization, logistics, compliance, and executive-level program support.

## 10. Operational Assessment

### Apparent strengths

**Deep range-domain specialization.**  
The combination of radar, telemetry, optics, TSPI/ACMI, mission control, safety systems and flight-test instrumentation indicates meaningful specialization rather than generic engineering support.

**End-to-end mission support.**  
IntegrITS covers design, requirements, integration, test, operations, maintenance, data analysis, program management, sustainment and compliance. This supports a lifecycle-oriented contracting model.

**Strong alignment with defense customers.**  
The company’s veteran workforce, military-installation footprint, SeaPort-NxG positioning and range-service portfolio make its defense-market focus clear.

**Technical and administrative integration.**  
Customers can potentially obtain engineering, cybersecurity, acquisition, logistics, training, budgeting, quality and program-office support through one contractor.

### Information gaps on the website

The website does not provide detailed public information on:

- Revenue, employee count or company size
- Specific current contract awards
- Contract values or periods of performance
- Detailed project case studies
- Formal customer-success metrics
- Technical certifications
- CAGE, UEI or other registration identifiers
- Security-clearance capabilities
- Quality certifications such as ISO or CMMI
- Named technology partners
- Detailed commercial or healthcare engagements
- Current vacancies hosted directly on the IntegrITS domain

These omissions do not imply the company lacks these qualifications; they are simply not disclosed in the reviewed pages.

## 11. Bottom-Line Characterization

IntegrITS is best characterized from its website as a **small, veteran-oriented defense professional-services contractor with concentrated expertise in military test ranges, flight-test and range instrumentation, DoD engineering, program management, enterprise IT and cybersecurity**.

Its most credible and differentiated public positioning is not general-purpose IT consulting. It is the integration of:

> **Range operations + instrumentation + systems engineering + test and evaluation + program execution + IT/cybersecurity.**

The company appears especially well aligned to customers requiring personnel who can operate within military installations, acquisition frameworks, test organizations, mission environments and regulated government programs. Its website presents a mature services organization with a long operating history, a stable executive structure and a workforce culture deliberately aligned with military experience and mission execution.
