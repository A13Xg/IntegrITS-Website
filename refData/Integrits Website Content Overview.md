# **Strategic Capabilities and Operational Assessment of Integrits Corporation**

## **Corporate Infrastructure, Governance, and Business Identity**

Founded in January 2000 by Clarence Carter, Integrits Corporation (stylized as IntegrITS) operates as a Veteran-Owned Small Business (VOSB) and defense contractor headquartered in San Diego, California1. The enterprise delivers specialized technical services across range engineering, test and evaluation (T\&E), program management, and enterprise information technology (IT) and cybersecurity2. Derived from the Latin term *integritas*—denoting technical soundness, uprightness, and an unimpaired condition—the corporate philosophy centers on strict quality standards, ethical business practices, and long-term alignment with U.S. Department of Defense (DoD) programs6.  
Integrits Corporation maintains an active entity profile in the federal System for Award Management (SAM) registered through September 20267. The organization operates under Unique Entity Identifier (UEI) QVMYLK59N3G2 and Commercial and Government Entity (CAGE) Code 1LVF22. The company's primary North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code is 541330 (Engineering Services), complemented by secondary NAICS codes 336611 (Ship Building and Repairing) and 334111 (Computer and Peripheral Equipment Manufacturing)2. Beyond its VOSB designation, federal contract records document Integrits as a self-certified Small Disadvantaged Business and Black American Owned Minority Business Enterprise2.

## **Federal Contracting Profile, Vehicles, and Programmatic Execution**

Integrits Corporation has secured more than $102.1 million in cumulative federal contract value, divided between $90.5 million in prime contract awards and $11.6 million in subcontract awards2. The Department of the Navy represents the firm's primary federal contracting partner, comprising nearly 100% of its prime contract awards2. Within naval commands, the Naval Information Warfare Systems Command (NAVWAR) accounts for approximately 65% of overall awarded contract volume, establishing Integrits as a direct technical service provider for naval command, control, communications, computers, and intelligence (C4I) infrastructure2.  
To maintain flexible procurement options for government agencies, Integrits executes business across diverse contracting vehicles and fee structures3. Contract types managed by the firm include Cost Plus Fixed Fee (CPFF), Cost Plus Award Fee (CPAF), Time and Materials (T\&M), Firm Fixed Price (FFP), and FFP Level of Effort3.

A central operational commitment for Integrits is its execution under the Joint Range Technical Services Contract (J-Tech II)9. Personnel deployed under J-Tech II provide systems administration, virtual server configuration, software development, and network instrumentation at Edwards Air Force Base and Nellis Air Force Base, supporting test environments for warfighting aircraft, weapons systems, and flight crews9.  
At Pearl Harbor, Hawaii, Integrits maintains a direct prime presence through a $14.9 million IDIQ contract providing operations and maintenance (O\&M) for the Mobile At-Sea Sensor System (MATSS) vessel17. This specialized vessel platform provides mobile ocean-based sensor data collection and range measurement capabilities for naval fleet testing3. Additionally, Integrits supports NAVWAR's Tactical Communications Program Office (PMW 170), providing budget execution analysis, risk mitigation, schedule tracking, and acquisition documentation aligned with the Defense Acquisition System18.

## **Domain Capabilities and Technical Service Lines**

Integrits Corporation structures its core capabilities into three primary service sectors: Range Engineering & Technical Services, Program Management Services, and Information Technology & Cybersecurity Services1. These service lines encompass full system life-cycle disciplines, tailored to military commands, healthcare organizations, Fortune 500 companies, and commercial IT developers5.

### **Range Engineering & Technical Services**

Integrits operates, maintains, and modernizes military test range instrumentation systems required for Research, Development, Test, and Evaluation (RDT\&E) as well as Developmental Testing/Operational Testing (DT/OT) missions1. Range operations focus on maintaining real-time data integrity across complex tracking environments19.  
Technical range engineering capabilities encompass direct operational maintenance of tracking radars, telemetry processing systems, optical tracking systems, aircraft Time Space Position Information (TSPI), and Air Combat Maneuvering Instrumentation (ACMI) pods1. Range personnel manage range safety systems, communications networks, and mission control centers1. Field support spans in-shop ACMI pod repair and maintenance to Pacific Theater Ballistic Missile Defense (BMD) tracking support at the Pacific Missile Range Facility (PMRF)11. Technical oversight covers Independent Verification & Validation (IV\&V), Test Readiness Reviews (TRR), airspace utilization analysis, flight test instrumentation planning, and post-test data collection and analysis19.

### **Program Management Services**

Integrits delivers programmatic support tailored to Echelon II military commands, Program Executive Offices, and commercial partners18. These capabilities ensure that federal acquisitions comply with statutory, regulatory, and policy guidelines under DoD/SECNAV 5000 standards18.  
Program management offerings include Program Management Office (PMO) staffing, resource planning, policy formulation, and execution report analysis for budget and cost control18. Acquisition capabilities cover Integrated Logistics Support (ILS), Life Cycle Sustainment, Quality Assurance/Quality Control (QA/QC), and Supply Chain Management (SCM) policy development20. Furthermore, Integrits designs business continuity plans, monitors program performance metrics, and develops specialized Computer-Based Training (CBT) modules and operational curricula for defense applications20.

### **Information Technology & Cybersecurity Services**

Integrits provides enterprise-level IT engineering, infrastructure deployment, cybersecurity authorization, and defense-in-depth operational support for federal and commercial architectures1.  
Cybersecurity services focus on governance under the Risk Management Framework (RMF), conducting security Assessments and Authorizations (A\&A), vulnerability scans, risk mitigations, and Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) integration2. Enterprise IT engineering covers Command, Control, Communications, Computers, and Intelligence (C4I), Command and Control (C2), Common Operational Picture (COP), Network Operations Center (NOC) design, LAN/WAN installation, and AWS Cloud operations2. Software integration capabilities include Customer Relationship Management (CRM) application development, Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP), Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA), tactical data link (TADIL) integration, and NMCI deployment support2. Operational support services manage Remedy-based help desks, seat management, disaster recovery planning, and facilities hardware maintenance2.

## **Strategic Synthesis and Market Positioning**

Integrits Corporation's business model demonstrates a focused positioning strategy within the defense professional services market1. By combining range technical support with enterprise IT modernization and C4ISR program management, the company occupies a specialized niche servicing both live test range environments and enterprise defense networks2. An evaluation of the firm's operational structure reveals several strategic insights regarding its growth trajectory and risk profile.  
Maintaining a staff composition where over 70% of personnel are military veterans serves as a direct operational risk control mechanism10. Contracting work at secure facilities—such as Edwards AFB or NAVWAR headquarters—requires personnel with active Secret or Top Secret security clearances and specialized operational knowledge9. Securing initial personnel clearances for civilian hires often introduces onboarding delays that impede contract execution18. By drawing from a pool of military veterans who already possess active clearances and familiarity with defense acquisition protocols, Integrits reduces labor startup lag, accelerates program deployment, and ensures immediate compliance with DoD operational security standards10.  
Furthermore, Integrits’ contract history illustrates a calculated transition from sub-tier range execution toward prime contract ownership2. While sustaining stable subcontracting revenue via joint vehicles like J-Tech II, the company has leveraged its SeaPort-NxG prime access and dedicated awards—such as the $14.9 million MATSS vessel contract—to build direct past performance credentials with naval systems commands2. This approach positions the company to compete effectively for larger prime technical contracts while preserving revenue stability through multi-year defense range programs2.  
Finally, active recruitment profiles reflect the ongoing convergence of physical test range operations with cloud infrastructure and cybersecurity frameworks9. Historically, range instrumentation relied on localized, air-gapped hardware configurations1. However, job requisitions for AWS Cloud leads and RMF systems administrators at Edwards AFB demonstrate that test ranges are increasingly adopting virtualized, cloud-hosted architectures9. Integrits’ established domain experience in operating physical range instrumentation (radar, optics, telemetry) combined with enterprise cloud administration and RMF authorization places the organization in an advantageous position as the Department of Defense continues to modernize its operational test and evaluation ranges into networked, real-time data environments9.

## **Conclusions**

Integrits Corporation has established a durable operational footprint within the defense services sector1. Grounded by strong veteran representation, steady prime contracting vehicles like SeaPort-NxG, and deep integration into Navy C4ISR and Air Force test range programs, the company maintains high institutional stability2. As military requirements shift toward cloud-based range data processing, automated cybersecurity compliance under RMF, and modernized C4I integration, Integrits remains well positioned to expand its prime technical services across Department of Defense test, training, and operational commands2.
