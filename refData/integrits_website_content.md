### 1. Range Engineering & Technical Services
We operate, maintain, and modernize critical research, developmental, and operational test ranges (RDT&E / DT / OT) for warfighters across land, air, and sea.
* **Instrumentation & Tracking:** Radar systems, telemetry processing, optical tracking, and flight test instrumentation.
* **Air Combat Systems:** ACMI pods, Time Space Position Information (TSPI), and in-shop pod maintenance.
* **Missile Defense & Range Safety:** Ballistic Missile Defense (BMD) tracking, mission control centers, and airspace utilization analysis.
* **Test Verification:** Independent Verification & Validation (IV&V) and Test Readiness Reviews (TRR).

### 2. Program Management & Advisory
We provide high-level acquisition, financial, and strategic management to Echelon II commands and Program Executive Offices (PEOs).
* **PMO Support & Acquisition:** DoD/SECNAV 5000 compliance, requirements analysis, and life-cycle sustainment.
* **Financial & Risk Management:** Cost control, budget execution analysis, and program risk mitigation.
* **Logistics & Quality:** Integrated Logistics Support (ILS), Supply Chain Management (SCM), and QA/QC policies.
* **Training Solutions:** Computer-Based Training (CBT) modules, operational curricula, and process improvement.

### 3. Enterprise IT & Cybersecurity
We build, secure, and operate scalable IT infrastructures that safeguard critical defense networks and enterprise operations.
* **Cybersecurity Compliance:** Risk Management Framework (RMF) Assessment & Authorization (A&A), vulnerability scanning, and PKI integration.
* **Cloud & Infrastructure:** AWS Cloud architecture, VM server management, LAN/WAN engineering, and NOC operations.
* **C4ISR Solutions:** Command & Control (C2), Common Operational Picture (COP), and Tactical Data Link (TADIL) integration.
* **Managed Services:** Enterprise help-desk operations (Remedy ITSM), seat management, and disaster recovery.

## 🗺️ Operational Footprint

IntegrITS maintains active engineering teams and site offices co-located at major defense installations nationwide:

* **San Diego, CA (Headquarters):** Central administrative hub supporting NAVWAR, NIWC Pacific, and PEO C4I (PMW 170).
* **Edwards Air Force Base, CA:** Flight test instrumentation, AWS cloud infrastructure, and J-Tech II range systems administration.
* **Nellis Air Force Base & NTTR, NV:** Tactical range support, operational test environments, and server engineering.
* **Pacific Missile Range Facility (PMRF), Kauai, HI:** Telemetry, optics, tracking radars, and missile defense test support.
* **Pearl Harbor Shipyard, HI:** Operations and maintenance (O&M) for the Mobile At-Sea Sensor System (MATSS) vessel.
* **Regional Field Operations:** Hill Air Force Base (UT), Fort Irwin (CA), Phoenix (AZ), and Raleigh (NC).
